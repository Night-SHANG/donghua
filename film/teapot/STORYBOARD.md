Logline: A proud little teapot blows a bubble, gets carried away by a much bigger one, and learns to enjoy a tiny bubble instead.
World: hand-painted garden tea patio; warm cream paper, pale sage wall/foliage, muted terracotta ground. Hero is mint/teal enamel, apricot lid and short feet. No lettering, no 3D, no digital gradients. One silhouette body, spout right, handle left, two expressive eyes. Simple short feet; no elaborate animal anatomy.
Motif: bubble as a circle of delight; opening circular reveal on bubble, ending circular close on the hero and little bubble.
Arc: curious → proud → determined → excited → nervous → startled → dazed → cautious → relieved/delighted.
